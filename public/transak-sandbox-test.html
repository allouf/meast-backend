5. What "success" looks like

  • Quote returns a `conversionPrice`, `cryptoAmount`, `quoteId`.
  • Sign-URL returns a full `widget_url` and a `signature`.
  • Opening the widget loads Transak's UI without "invalid signature" errors.
  • Completing a sandbox purchase fires a webhook to `/api/v1/wallet/webhooks/transak` and creates a row in `transak_orders` (check phpMyAdmin or `tinker`).
